OBS setup

Airlock sits on this PC between OBS and the platform. Point OBS at Airlock, not at Twitch.

  1. Settings → Stream → Service: Custom.
  2. Server: rtmp://127.0.0.1:1935/live
  3. Stream key: airlock. This is only the local hop. The platform key stays in the OS keychain and is never typed into OBS.

Use a high bitrate on this local hop. Airlock passes video through untouched and only re-encodes audio.

  • Output mode: Advanced. Encoder: NVENC, or x264 on a machine without it.
  • Rate control: CBR. Bitrate: 15000 Kbps at 1080p. Keyframe interval: 2 seconds.
  • Audio: 48 kHz, 320 Kbps.
